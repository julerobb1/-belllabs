Research archives

Bell Labs published its work. This page points at public collections and at the articles on this site. We do not hold a private document locker, and the form that used to sit here did not send mail.

Nobel-recognized work

  • Transistor — Bardeen, Brattain, and Shockley; Nobel Prize in Physics 1956. Official citation: Nobel Prize 1956.
  • Cosmic microwave background — Penzias and Wilson at Holmdel; Nobel Prize in Physics 1978. Nobel Prize 1978.
  • Information theory — Claude Shannon, “A Mathematical Theory of Communication,” Bell System Technical Journal, 1948. Read BSTJ on the Internet Archive.
  • Charge-coupled device — Boyle and Smith; Nobel Prize in Physics 2009. Nobel Prize 2009.
