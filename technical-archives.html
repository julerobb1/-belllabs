Technical archives

Pointers to historical Bell System documents. This site does not store the PDFs themselves; the links go to libraries and public archives.

Journals and books

Bell System Technical Journal (1922–1983)

Papers on transmission, switching, radio, materials, and computing from Bell Labs authors.

Browse BSTJ on the Internet Archive

Transmission systems

Bell Laboratories’ Transmission Systems for Communications (several editions, including 1964 and later) is the textbook treatment of FDM, radio, and coaxial plant used on Long Lines.

Search library catalogs or the Internet Archive by that title; we do not host a scan.

L-carrier and microwave

System descriptions for L1/L3/L4/L5 and TD-2/TH radios appear in BSTJ special issues and in Bell System Practices. Start with the articles on this site, then the BSTJ issues they cite:
